# Visual direction: guidance

Write a short direction for each site (10–15 lines in `00_BRIEF.md`), presented as "a proposal, feel free to refine it".

- **A concept drawn from the topic** (networks: "packets travelling between layers"; mechanics: "engineer's notebook, measuring instruments").
- **One signature motif** (a discreet topic-related animation on the home page). Everything else sober.
- **Meaningful colours**: one per recurring notion, constant across badges, diagrams and widgets; never meaning by colour alone (label or legend too); orange/red reserved for pitfalls, green for validations. Give hex values for light and dark, WCAG AA checked.
- **Plots and canvases follow the theme**: no black area on a light page.
- **Typography**: name three fonts (body, headings/UI, monospace).
- **Avoid**: identical cards everywhere, generic SaaS kit, decorative gradients, grey shadows everywhere, spaced-out capital labels above headings, fade-ins on every section, full-page grid patterns, cream + terracotta, black + single neon accent.
